THE RULES OF PARTING

One move.
Two consequences.

←A
B→
  1. Choose any two pucks.

    All eight are shared. The line between your pair sets the direction; you choose the force.

  2. Drag the force slider. Release.

    Both pucks launch away from each other at the same speed. Watch the collisions settle, then pass the mouse.

  3. Score through your opening.

    Amber scores left, Blue scores right, whoever took the shot. Scored pucks disappear, taking their possible pairings with them.

Most points after 24 turns wins. The game ends sooner if fewer than two pucks remain. Equal scores are a draw.
